# Group and business naming directions

These are naming concepts. Domain, company-name, and trademark availability have not been checked. The group selected **Alloy** as its parent brand. Existing site and film branding will be updated together in the branding rollout.

## Naming objective

A parent brand for complementary AI, web, and desktop engineers. The name should suggest purposeful engineering and collaboration, work in a client proposal, and allow the group to deliver beyond any single project. Prefer a short name that does not depend on the team always having three members.

## Shortlist

| Concept | What it expresses | Suggested business presentation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Groundframe | A solid foundation and a coherent structure connecting disciplines | Groundframe — Applied AI & Product Engineering |
| Prooflane | An engineering path guided by evidence and evaluation | Prooflane — Private AI. Measurable Progress. |
| Commonframe | A shared engineering identity rather than disconnected individual tools | Commonframe — Independent Minds. Shared Engineering. |
| Clearbound | Clear permissions and intelligible system boundaries | Clearbound — AI Built Around Your Boundaries. |

## Additional concepts

| Concept | Association | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Measured Works | Evaluation discipline and practical delivery | Credible but less compact as a wordmark |
| Layerfield | AI, interfaces, and infrastructure brought together | Abstract; needs a clear descriptor |
| Rootline | Foundations, continuity, and purposeful development | Broad; the tagline must explain the engineering offer |
| Framewell | Thoughtfully constructed systems and interfaces | Friendly; may read more like a design studio |
| Proof & Practice | Research translated into working implementation | Strong positioning; longer spoken and written name |
| Groundline Engineering | A dependable foundation for applied systems | Clear business association; less distinctive than Groundframe |
| Signal & Structure | Intelligence connected to useful software architecture | Editorial and expressive; long for compact product interfaces |
| Shared Standard | Collaboration through common quality expectations | Strong collective identity; harder to use as a single-word product brand |

## Recommendation

Groundframe is the strongest starting direction for a broad business identity: it can cover secure AI, full-stack products, and developer tooling without sounding like a single feature or tool.

Prooflane is the better direction if evaluation-led AI becomes the core commercial specialization.

Commonframe best emphasizes the group as a collaborative entity.

## Logo briefs to develop after selection

- Groundframe: a compact structural monogram or framing symbol; simple geometry and a confident wordmark.
- Prooflane: a continuous path or precise convergence mark; avoid a generic shield/checkmark treatment.
- Commonframe: distinct elements forming one shared frame; avoid a symbol that requires exactly three founders.

Use the chosen identity consistently across the landing page, proposals, project presentations, demo videos, and email signatures. Keep the existing project names underneath the parent brand.

## Selected direction: Alloy

Alloy expresses complementary AI, product, and desktop engineering strengths forming one shared identity. Working descriptor: Applied AI & Product Engineering. Suggested tagline: Different strengths. Better systems. Future logo direction: distinct structural elements joining into a cohesive mark, without requiring exactly three founders.
